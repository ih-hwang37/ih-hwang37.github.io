Injune Hwang

PhD Researcher · University of Edinburgh

Hello! I am a PhD researcher in Inverse Problems and Machine Learning at the University of Edinburgh, supervised by Prof. Mike E. Davies .

I am interested in sensing theorems for Unsupervised Learning in Inverse Problems, including Equivariant Imaging , drawing on ideas from differential geometry and representation theory. I also develop self-supervised learning algorithms for inverse problems in biophysics, including Cryo-EM/ET, and medical imaging.

Before starting my PhD, I worked on dynamic graph-based machine learning methods for 3D freehand ultrasound with Prof. Stephen Mellon . I studied Mathematics (BA,MMATH) at the University of Oxford, specialising in differential geometry, algebraic topology, and geometric machine learning. As an undergraduate, I worked with Prof. Jason Lotay on mean curvature flow and geometric analysis.
