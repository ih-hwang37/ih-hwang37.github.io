Injune Hwang

PhD Researcher · University of Edinburgh

I am a PhD researcher at the University of Edinburgh, advised by Prof. Michael E. Davies .

My research focuses on inverse problems and mathematical approaches to machine learning inspired by geometry, topology, and analysis, with applications in medical imaging and biophysics.

Before joining Edinburgh, I studied Pure Mathematics at the University of Oxford, specialising in differential geometry, algebraic topology, and geometric machine learning.
