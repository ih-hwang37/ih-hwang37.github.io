Injune Hwang

PhD Researcher · University of Edinburgh

I am a PhD researcher at the University of Edinburgh, advised by Prof. Mike E. Davies .

My research focuses on inverse problems in imaging, developing mathematical theory and machine-learning algorithms inspired by geometry, topology, and analysis. I am particularly interested in applications to biophysics, including Cryo-EM and Cryo-ET, as well as medical imaging.

Before joining Edinburgh, I studied Pure Mathematics at the University of Oxford, specialising in differential geometry, algebraic topology, and geometric machine learning.
